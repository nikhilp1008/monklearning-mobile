## A correction, and the defect behind it

An earlier version of this report said four assets were refused because the
`concepts` table lacked their concepts, and called it a gap in the concept
catalogue. **That was wrong, and the cause was a bug in this ingest.**

`load_concept_tables` used a raw `supabase.table('concepts').select(...)`.
PostgREST caps an unpaged select at 1000 rows and reports the truncation
nowhere — no error, no warning, a short list that looks plausible. The table
holds 1,172. All four concepts existed, were `active = true`, and sat in
exactly the chapter the manifest named; they were among the 172 rows never
fetched.

    The Flower: Floral Parts, Symmetry and Ovary Position
      69c6b0a6-080d-4c7e-a8a3-94069522ece6   the-flower-floral-parts-symmetry-and-ovary-position
    Earthworm: Circulatory, Excretory and Reproductive Systems
      17669e53-7f2b-4cc4-90a8-3f0bad6dabce   earthworm-circulatory-excretory-and-reproductive-systems

`app/db.fetch_all` already pages, and its docstring already names `concepts`
as a table that exceeds the ceiling. The rule was written down and not
applied. The ingest now uses it, and a test reads a 1,172-row fake through a
double that truncates at 1000 the way PostgREST does — the previous fake
ignored `range()` entirely, so it could not tell a paging reader from a
truncating one and the suite passed either way.

Nothing was renamed, re-homed or parked to work around it. The three
earthworm assets stayed on the concept they were authored for.
