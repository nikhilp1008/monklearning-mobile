- p. 1017 Practice 5 A "All movements are not locomotion": ambiguous ("not all movements are locomotion").
- p. 1013/1020/1027 "reliably tested", "almost every year", "perennial", "at least one assertion-reason": unlabelled frequency claims (labelled where numeric, see above; others left as printed).
