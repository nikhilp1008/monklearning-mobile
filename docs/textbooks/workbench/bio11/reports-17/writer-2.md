## Suspected book errors (page, quote, why)
- p. 1033/1034/1038 axial 80: listed parts (skull 22, column 26, sternum 1, ribs 12 pairs = 73) never state ossicles 6 + hyoid 1; p. 1034 "three tiny bones of the middle ear" is per ear; cheat sheet omits the hyoid. NCERT counts the skull as 29 (22 + 6 + 1).
- p. 1034 "Tendon/long-bone landmarks": the scapula is a flat bone and the acromion is not a tendon landmark (mislabel). "the only cells it contains, the chondrocytes" (also chondroblasts). "red marrow (in the heads of long bones)": adults also flat bones.
- p. 1034 "The only freely movable skull bone is the mandible"; p. 1038 "only mandible movable": ossicles/hyoid are movable head bones (not counted in the 22); fine for the skull proper.
- p. 1035/1038 sternum-rib joint "cartilaginous": only rib 1 (and the costal cartilages); sternocostal joints 2-7 are synovial. Q4 key (c) is the standard NEET answer.
- p. 1035 synchondrosis "ossifies after puberty", "present only in children until puberty": growth plates fuse to about the mid-20s; some synchondroses (rib 1) are permanent.
- p. 1038 pro-tip "Almost every NEET/CUET joint question ..."; p. 1045 "The most-tested swap"; p. 1040 "Almost every year", "Pound-for-pound one of the most NEET-rewarding"; p. 1033 "high-scoring", "reliably": unsupported frequency/superlatives.
- p. 1041 "NEET increasingly frames disorders through symptoms"; p. 1042 "it is tetany, never the other two" (overgeneral); "answers almost every exam item".
- p. 1043 "Tetany is a failure of the calcium signal that Topic 02 showed triggers contraction; too little Ca2+ and the muscle mis-fires": misleading. Tetany is low extracellular Ca2+ raising nerve/membrane excitability; the intracellular SR Ca2+ signal is not what fails.

- p. 1041 "Muscular dystrophy typically appears in childhood" (Duchenne; other forms adult).
