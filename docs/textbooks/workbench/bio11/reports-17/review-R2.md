FALSE: Topic 02 (p. 1030, "How a whole muscle grades its force") states the all-or-none law, recruitment of motor units, faster firing and that "individual twitches fuse (summation)", and gives oxygen debt and lactic acid. Only the twitch phases, the word tetanus and the finer fatigue mechanism are new. Also the kalam and first sentence say "The chapter states that a muscle fibre obeys the all-or-none law": true (Topic 02). Fix [NEW]: "This is true for a single fibre, but it raises an obvious question: if each fibre is all-or-none, how does the whole muscle produce the smooth gradations of force you see when you slowly lift a glass versus lift a heavy bucket? Topic 02 gives the answer in one paragraph: the whole muscle grades its force by recruiting more motor units and by firing each more rapidly, so that twitches summate. This topic develops it: the phases of a twitch, summation, tetanus and fatigue, which the chapter names only in passing (and tetanus not at all)." Kind: false never-covers claim. Source: book. Confidence: certain.
