**Where I departed from the book's words.** The header comment lists these.
- **RGR versus r:** the chapter's formula, final minus initial size over initial size times Δt, is called the average (ratio form). The exact relative rate is r in W₁ = W₀e^(rt), the efficiency index. The two agree only for small changes, and the point says r carries time⁻¹, so "RGR is dimensionless" is not repeated.
- **Sigmoid curve:** geometric growth is exponential and J-shaped while resources are unlimited, and it is limited resources that flatten the whole-life curve into the S-shaped sigmoid. Arithmetic growth is a straight line.
- **Plant versus animal growth:** plant growth is open, indeterminate, intrinsic and localized, and leaves, flowers and fruits are determinate. The book's "an animal grows all over and then stops" and "these factories never retire" are dropped.
- **Hormones:**
  - GA, not auxin or cytokinin, breaks dormancy, and ABA enforces it.
  - Stomatal closure is ABA's alone.
  - Abscission is the ethylene job in the exam, with ABA's headline roles being dormancy and stomatal closure.
  - "The ratio, not the amounts" and "the bigger partner wins" are dropped.
  - The "Indole = natural" mnemonic is replaced by listing the natural and synthetic auxins.
  - Cytokinin is not called basic.
- **Photoperiod and vernalisation:**
  - LDP examples are only spinach, radish, lettuce and *Hyoscyamus niger*, because wheat, sugar beet and barley are listed under both flowering needs.
  - Dahlia and wild kidney bean are omitted as not NCERT.
  - Day-neutral plants flower in "essentially any photoperiod".
  - A defoliated plant "cannot respond to the photoperiod", not "cannot flower under any photoperiod".
  - Devernalisation needs heat given soon after the cold.
  - The cold/disease-resistance claim and "cold means vernalisation, full stop" are dropped.
- **Dormancy:**
  - The GA to ABA balance is described as the chapter's account of the switch, not as a rule for every case.
  - The ABI-proteins sentence, "seasonal dormancy", the Lathyrus hot-water example and the line "ABA broken down" as fact are dropped.
  - Afterripening says the book explains it by falling ABA.
- **Phytochrome:** Pr, Pfr, the wavelengths, reversibility and the canopy are used. The "Pfr promotes flowering in LDP and inhibits it in SDP" sentence and dark reversion as the night-length timer are not used, and elongation under a canopy is credited to the low Pfr:Pr ratio.
- **Nastic movements:** the "tropism is slow, growth-based" table row and "seismonasty has no circadian rhythm" are not used. Both movements are described as turgor changes at the pulvinus.
- **What NEET Actually Asks:** the shares are the book's estimate, with no years or counts. The "decides more marks than any others" and "most frequent single trap" superlatives are dropped.
