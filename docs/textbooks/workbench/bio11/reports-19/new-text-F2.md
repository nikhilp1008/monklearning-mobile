2. What the chapter states, last sentence [R1-1, R2-7a]: "The chapter traces the thyroid axis (TRH → TSH → T<sub>3</sub>/T<sub>4</sub>, with negative feedback on the hypothalamus and the pituitary) and outlines the gonadal axis (GnRH → FSH/LH → sex steroids, with inhibin feeding back).
