**Where I departed from the book's words**
- **Root versus stem:** the point gives "no nodes or internodes, no leaves" and makes the test nodes with scale-leaves or buds in leaf axils, not "no buds". The storage-root "tapers" check, "fibrous anchors more securely" and "stilt and prop roots found only in mangroves" are not used.
- **Didynamous and Fabaceae 9+1:** didynamous is left out, because Salvia, the book's example, has two fertile stamens. Fabaceae stamens are "usually nine fused and one free".
- **Locule rule:** the "locule count alone separates the three families" claim is not used.
- **Fruits:** a drupe is given with mango only, not coconut. The pepo and cypsela are not called "true fruit, ovary only". The cypsela is described as a dry, indehiscent, one-seeded fruit from an inferior ovary. The hesperidium "unicellular hairs", "composite fruit" and the mango "superior or inferior" are not used.
- **Scutellum, phylloclade and phyllode:**
  - The scutellum is said of the maize and wheat grains, not of all monocots.
  - Euphorbia is not given as a phylloclade; Opuntia is the phylloclade, Asparagus the cladode and Acacia the phyllode, and they are kept as stem against petiole.
- **"Never covers" claims:** the false "never covers" claims are not repeated. The point gives the order of the semi-technical description without counting its steps, since the book's counts shift between ten, eleven and twelve.
- **Bulb:** a bulb is an underground shoot, so "all three are underground stems" is not repeated. The book's "nodes not present" for the bulb is not used.
- **Ovule types:** the five ovule types (B4) are left out. NCERT Class 11 does not teach them, and the campylotropous row contradicts itself.
- **Asteraceae:** the capitulum has no order of opening, because the book says both "centripetal" and "racemose acropetal". A ray floret is "usually pistillate, sometimes neutral", and its separate formula is not used.
- **Additional D reverse routine:** the "given" includes actinomorphic and bisexual, which the book's Step 1 reads but its description omits.
- **Superlatives:** "single most-examined", "guaranteed trap" and "every venation question is built to tempt the reversal" are dropped.
- **Stamen cohesion:** epipetalous is an attachment and diadelphous a cohesion, not both "cohesion" as the traps table has it.
