## Suspected book errors (page, quote, why)
- p. 923 D.2 "Beyond a certain heart rate (~170-180/min in a fit adult) ... SV actually falls" vs the table (same page) and D.1 which take SV 120-130 mL at 180-200/min: internal tension.
- p. 924 D practice 5 and p. 908: SA node "intrinsic ~90-100/min", slowed by vagus to "~70-75/min"; the main text elsewhere gives the pacemaker as 70-75/min (both kept; pp. 906, 908, 924). 90-100 x 70 mL is 6.3-7 L/min, so the question's premise is only about the vagal brake.
- p. 926 E.1 asks "What blood group phenotypes are possible" but answers only Rh genotypes/phenotypes.
- p. 925 "Rh-negative phenotype: genotype dd" is the textbook simplification (RHD deletion in many populations; Rh is multi-gene); p. 926 E.2 "Rhogam" brand name.
- p. 928 F practice 3 answer lists the thoracic duct territory as "abdomen, lower limbs, and left side of the thorax, head, and neck", omitting the left upper limb printed in the p. 927 table.
- p. 929 G table "Thalassaemia (defective globin chain synthesis -> microcytic anaemia)" listed only under haemolytic; p. 930 note "covered in greater detail in Genetics": Class 11 has no Genetics chapter (Class 12 Principles of Inheritance).
- p. 931 G practice 5 and question: "why might one cause neural tube defects ... while the other does not": low maternal B12 is also an independent NTD risk factor; the book's answer does not claim otherwise but the question's premise is overgeneral.
- p. 932 H table: "Haemophilia A/B ... petechiae are NOT typical" fine; "Von Willebrand disease ... most common inherited bleeding disorder" fine; p. 933 answer 3 "affects approximately 1% of the population" (about 1% by laboratory criteria, symptomatic cases far fewer: overstated unlabelled). p. 933 answer 4 "Platelet count must fall to ... < 20,000/mm3 before clotting time becomes noticeably prolonged": unsourced threshold. p. 934 answer 5 factor VIII half-life "about 12 hours bound / about 2 hours free": unlabelled figures. - p. 934 I "In recent NEET and AIPMT papers ... seven zones", "very often", "tested at least once per NEET cycle": unlabelled frequency claims (labelled by us).
- p. 936 Archetype 3: "If A is false, the answer is (d) (both false) when R is also false": the standard fourth option is "A false, R true"; the book prints "(d) Both false" in Archetype 3, Q9 and Q10 (pp. 936, 941, 942). Copied, reported.
