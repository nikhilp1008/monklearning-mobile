## Flags, most serious first

1. A shared Introduction (p. 915), "the two-pathway cascade the chapter summarises in four beats". FALSE-ish: Topic 01 already names the intrinsic and extrinsic pathways, tissue thromboplastin, Ca2+ at several steps, factors VIII, IX, X, Xa. Fix [NEW]: "Additional topic A numbers every clotting factor (I to XIII) and writes out the intrinsic and extrinsic pathways factor by factor; the chapter names both pathways and a few factors but not the full list." Kind: never-covers claim. Source: book. Certain.
2. A second paragraph and kalam, "four-step sequence: prothrombinase, prothrombin, thrombin, fibrinogen ... the correct order". Not what the chapter says and not an order (two are substrates). Chapter: trigger (platelet factor 3 + thromboplastin), prothrombinase forms, thrombin from prothrombin, fibrin from fibrinogen. Fix [NEW]: "The chapter presents the clotting cascade in four steps: a trigger (platelet factor 3 and tissue thromboplastin), formation of prothrombinase, thrombin from prothrombin, and fibrin from fibrinogen. That is the correct skeleton of the final common pathway, but NEET and CBSE ask for the numbered factors that feed into it (the book's estimate from past papers, not official figures)." Kalam: same first sentence. Certain.
3. FALSE: Topic 02 names both, with their veins (left and right subclavian) and the thoracic duct draining "the bulk". Fix [NEW] (F): "The chapter names the thoracic duct (into the left subclavian vein) and the right lymphatic duct (into the right subclavian vein) but gives no territory for each, nor the cisterna chyli. Shared intro [NEW]: "Additional topic F maps which body regions each of the two lymphatic ducts drains, a direct NEET recall question." Certain.
4. H Introduction (p. 931) and shared intro, "haemophilia and thrombocytopenia (which the chapter mentions only as names)", "does not compare bleeding time with clotting time, does not name the three primary bleeding disorders by type". PARTLY FALSE: Topic 01 describes thrombocytopenia (platelet fall, dengue, abnormal bleeding) and haemophilia A/B (X-linked recessive, VIII, IX, mechanism); A (answer 5) already contrasts bleeding and clotting time. Fix [NEW] shared intro: "Additional topic H sets haemophilia and thrombocytopenia, which the chapter describes only briefly, beside von Willebrand disease, and gives the normal values and meaning of bleeding time and clotting time, two tests NEET asks about." Fix [NEW] H: "The chapter describes thrombocytopenia (a fall in platelets, as in dengue) and haemophilia (factor VIII or IX deficiency) and names heparin as an anticoagulant. It does not cover von Willebrand disease or the normal values and meaning of bleeding time and clotting time (Additional topic A, answer 5, only uses the two terms). These are examinable distinctions." Certain.
