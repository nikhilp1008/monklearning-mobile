Fix [NEW]: "Double circulation is complete in crocodiles, birds and mammals (4-chambered heart). Amphibians and most other reptiles have a 3-chambered heart with <b>incomplete</b> double circulation: some mixing occurs." Same fix: T02 Practice 4 answer -> "(crocodiles, birds, mammals, 4-chambered)"; T02 MCQ4 nudge text fine; T02 "double circulation advantage ... never mix" (p. 899) add "in complete double circulation"; T03 (c) "complete partition of the ventricles in birds and mammals" -> "in crocodiles, birds and mammals"; T03 "to reach the left side of the heart, blood must first pass through the lungs ... always carries freshly oxygenated blood" -> add [NEW] "(in the adult; the foetus bypasses the lungs through shunts)"; T04 F "bird/mammal 4-ch complete" -> "crocodile/bird/mammal 4-ch complete". ## Overall judgement
Transcription of Topics 01-04 is word-for-word faithful and all numbers recompute. The book's own errors are the issue: the crocodile/complete-double-circulation contradiction, the SA node rate pair, the three-vs-four phases of the cardiac cycle, and a handful of overgeneral "only/all/always/single biggest" claims (flags 1-9, 14, 16). The MCQ keys are right. Figures follow the book's plates with sensible, honest schematics; the main risk is reading the 15.2 arrows. Key points already correct most of the book's looseness; only their page cites need fixing.
