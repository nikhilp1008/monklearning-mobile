**Where I departed from the book's words, so that no point carries a book mistake.** Each one is also listed in the header comment.
- **Anaphase chromosome number:** the point says S phase doubles DNA, not chromosome number. Once the sister chromatids separate, each counts as a chromosome, so the cell holds twice the number until it divides. It does not repeat "only appears to change in anaphase".
- **"Only diploid" claims:** no point says meiosis occurs only in diploid cells, only once in a life cycle, or that chiasmata occur only in meiosis.
- **Mitosis cell types:** mitosis is said to run in haploid and diploid cells, as the drone example shows, without the cheat sheet's "somatic" limit.
- **Synaptonemal complex:** described by its job (it holds the homologues in register), not as "DNA + protein".
- **Metaphase checkpoint:** it is the spindle checkpoint, which blocks anaphase until every chromosome is attached. APC/C is named as the machinery activated at anaphase onset, which degrades cyclin B, not as what "maintains" the checkpoint.
- **C values:** every one carries its baseline (diploid G1 = 2C, 4C after S, 2C after Meiosis I, 1C after Meiosis II). A gamete's 1C is stated as a quarter of the 4C in G2 and half of the 2C unreplicated diploid cell.
