## Suspected book errors (page, exact words, why)
- pp. 975, 976: "liver — urea/bile pigments" (practice 1 answer) and cheat sheet "liver (urea, bile pigments)": the liver makes urea, the kidney excretes it (Topic 02 p. 954 says so). Correct: liver excretes bile pigments, cholesterol, drugs; urea is excreted by the kidney.
- pp. 971, 976: "about 1-1.5 L per day" vs pp. 962, 968 "1-1.8 L" (NCERT: 1-1.5 L). Copied.
- p. 985: "converts two molecules of NH3 + CO2 + 3 ATP -> urea + 2 ADP + AMP + PPi + H2O": organic part balances (2 NH3 + CO2 = urea + H2O) but phosphate does not (3 ATP = 9 P; products 2 ADP + AMP + PPi = 7 P; 2 Pi missing, and water used in hydrolysis); net line "2 NH3 + CO2 + 3 ATP -> urea" lists no ATP products or fumarate; step 1 "NH3 + CO2 + 2ATP -> carbamoyl phosphate" omits 2 ADP + Pi. Schematic; second N really comes via aspartate (practice 2).
- p. 986 practice 4: "CPS-I is active only in the liver": also intestinal mucosa. p. 984: "Amino acids cannot be metabolised directly for energy" overgeneral (glutamate etc.).
- p. 976 Q4 / p. 971: "Only urea (and other nitrogenous wastes) leaves" / "Glucose and amino acids are not removed": overgeneral; dialysis also removes excess K+, phosphate etc. when above fluid level.
- p. 982 "AQP2 ... (only present when ADH is high)" and practice 2 "Low ADH: AQP2 ... absent from apical membrane": overstated, a basal amount remains (and AQP2 is in vesicles). 
- p. 983 C.1: "medullary gradient is intact" then "can also wash out part of the gradient" (internal tension).
- p. 979 practice 6: gradient "collapses toward ~300 mOsm/L ... the urine cannot be concentrated": loop diuretics reduce, not abolish.
- p. 980 "macula densa (early DCT...)": the macula densa is the end of the thick ascending limb at the start of the distal tubule. p. 980 "~10% ... 80-180 mmHg" is a textbook autoregulation range (mean arterial pressure).
- p. 990 F.1 "Na+ reabsorption ... obligatorily couples to K+ secretion": overgeneral.
- p. 976 "Mixing these is the #1 lost mark": unlabelled superlative (copied, not labelled). Tags [NEET]/[CUET]/[CBSE] are difficulty tags.
- p. 971 "Glomerulonephritis (nephritis)", p. 972 skin/lung lists match NCERT. Gradient 300-1200 mOsm/L matches NCERT.
