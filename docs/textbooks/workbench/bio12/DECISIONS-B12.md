## Standing rules carried over (founder)
1. **House style for every new subject** (Mathematics 11, 2026-11-10; the README extends it to Biology): every fraction stacked (`<frac><num>…</num><den>…</den></frac>`), no em dashes in the text. The checks (`book-rules.test.ts`) apply to Biology 12 as to Biology 11.
2. Word for word from the book; the book's mistakes are fixed in our copy only, after the expert review and the founder's call; the founder has so far always said "everything, optional included" (Biology 11 Chapter 1: "Apply all").
3. Exam citations carry no years, and a share the book gives is labelled "the book's estimate from past papers, not official figures" (the Maths rule). Class 12 prints years in the P topic of Chapters 1, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10 and 12 ("NEET 2019", "AIPMT 2015", "2000–2017"): each becomes "a recent NEET paper" (a law, a discovery or a census year that is a fact stays).

## Biology 12 layout calls (coordinator, 2026-11-16; to confirm with the founder)
4. **Topic numbers.** The Class 12 book has no "Sub-topic" numbers. Its topics are numbered sections 1, 2, 3 … in page order (Chapter 1: "Part I" to "Part IV"), and each becomes **Topic 01, 02, 03 …** with the book's own title (Chapter 1: the words after "Part I —", the dash and Part label dropped, as an addendum's title drops "Addendum A —"). Every printed "subtopic", "Part II" or "Chapter 4 (Ecological Succession)" cross-reference is renumbered by what it refers to, and each is listed. (Biology 11 Chapters 3, 4 and 7 had the same unnumbered shape: DECISIONS-B11 11.)
5. **Topic 00 only where the book has a front note.** No Class 12 chapter prints a "Sub-topic 00". **Chapter 8** opens (p. 409) with an unnumbered note "How to Use This Master Content"; it is kept as Topic 00 ("Start here", the Biology 11 Chapter 1 precedent, DECISIONS-B11 4) with one part and no eight sections. The founder may prefer to drop it: it adds nothing the chapter needs, and it says "all six NCERT sections" where five are listed (a book slip, reported).
6. **"Addendum P, What NEET Actually Asks"** is the LAST Additional topic of every chapter, lettered after the real addenda and any summary extra (Chemistry 11 decision 1, DECISIONS-B11 5): Chapter 1 G, 2 F, 3 G, 4 G, 5 G, 6 D, 7 H, 8 G, 9 D, 10 F, 11 F, 12 F, 13 F. Title "What NEET Actually Asks".
7. **Additional topics are lettered A, B, C …** in page order. The page header "Chapter N: … — Additional Topics" and the heading above the shared introduction ("Additional Topics — Human Reproduction", Chapter 4's "Overview", Chapter 6's "Additional Practice — Evolution", Chapter 10's "Addendum — Chapter 10 …") are not copied; the paragraph under it is the first paragraph(s) of Additional topic A's "Introduction". "Practice — Addendum A" is a part of that Additional topic. The titles are the words after "Addendum A —" with line-break hyphens removed ("Path-way" is "Pathway").
8. **Summary extras become their own Additional topic**, titled by the book's heading with a colon for any dash: Chapter 1 "Quick Reference Summary: Additional Topics" (F), Chapter 3 "Practice: Full Paper (All Addenda)" (F), Chapter 5 "Answer Key Summary" (F), Chapter 8 "Answer Key Summary: Additional Topics A to E" (F) (the Chemistry Summary Checklist and the Biology 11 Chapter 3 and 5 practice exercises were handled the same way, DECISIONS-B11 12).
9. **Scientific names in italics** (DECISIONS-B11 6), genus alone italic, ranks above the genus upright. Restriction enzyme names are copied as the page prints them (plain), not italicised by the genus letters.
10. **Genetics notation (new for Class 12).** Gene and allele symbols and genotypes are italic (`<i>Rr</i>`, `<i>I</i><sup><i>A</i></sup>`, `<i>X</i><sup><i>h</i></sup><i>Y</i>`), as the book prints them in mathematical italic and as every genetics text does; phenotypes, blood groups, generation labels (P, F<sub>1</sub>, F<sub>2</sub>), chromosome names and sex-chromosome pairs (XX, XY), karyotypes, proteins and DNA bases are upright. Ploidy is 2<i>n</i>, 3<i>n</i>. Ratios use "∶" (U+2236) as the book does. Punnett squares and genotype tables are `table` blocks. This is the one extension of the "`<i>` is for scientific names and maths" rule; if the founder prefers roman genotypes it is one search and replace.
11. **Equation variables, units and sequences.** Variables of population, ecology and Hardy–Weinberg equations are italic with real subscripts and superscripts; units upright, compound units with real superscripts and never a slash (g m<sup>−2</sup> yr<sup>−1</sup>); strand ends with U+2032 (5′, 3′); sequences upright capitals on one line. A "number over number" that is not a fraction is rewritten and listed (DECISIONS-B11, Slashes in Biology).
12. **MCQ and worked-example options are letters (a) to (d)** in this book, with "Correct: (c)" or "Answer: (b)" (the Class 11 book numbered them); the app keeps the book's letters, `correct` is the letter's position. The A-R key is "(a) both true, R explains A … (d) A false, R true"; "both false" is copied and reported.
13. **Chapter 12** prints its five topics as "Chapter 1" to "Chapter 5" (p. 659 onward); they are Topics 01 to 05 and each "Chapter 4 (…)" reference inside it is Topic 04. **Chapter 1** prints "Part I" to "Part IV" with eight unnumbered "Section N:" parts each.
14. **Figures.** All Class 12 figures are vector drawings (no raster images), numbered "Figure N.M", drawn on the page after their "(see next page)" caption. There is no figure in Chapter 4, none in any Additional topic, and no unnumbered drawing anywhere (a drawing census of pages 8 to 758 found drawings only on the numbered figure pages). **Figure 8.3** is a two-column comparison printed as a figure: it is drawn as a diagram with id `b12-08-fig-8.3` (the founder may prefer a `table`). **Figure 2.3** is printed inside Worked Example 4 and goes on that `ex` as `fig`. **Figure 12.4**'s return arrow is the corrected one (errata). Figure 3.1's green and red are carried by the labels (house diagrams use one highlight).
15. **Run-in lists.** The errata's "Remaining known issues" say several lists run on in one paragraph or inline (Chapters 2, 6, 8, 9, 11, 13; MCQ options in Chapters 6 and 8): they are written as proper lists and options, said once in each report. Layout slips (labels touching outlines, a heading alone at a page foot) are not copied into the app.
16. **Shelf wiring** (`CHAPTERS` in lib/textbooks.ts and `OPENS_ON_CHOICE`) is added only when a chapter is finished and checked (DECISIONS-B11 14); the shelf key is the catalogue name in lower case: `biology|12|biotechnology: principles and processes`, `biology|12|biotechnology and its applications` (lower-case "its", as `constants/onboarding-chapters.ts` has it).
