## Suspected book errors (page, exact quote, why, correction, confidence)
1. p. 648, 649, 650, 654, 655 (Archetype 4 and its example, Traps row, Q11, Key point 1): "(K − N)/K (equivalently 1 − N/K) is the fraction of carrying capacity still unused — the environmental factors that prevent the population from reaching its biotic potential. This is the 'environmental resistance' term" and Q11 "the term (1 − N/K) is called environmental resistance"; Traps "It is the room left — the environmental-resistance fraction". The term (1 − N/K) is the fraction of K still available; it falls as environmental resistance rises. "Environmental resistance" is the name for the limiting factors themselves (Chapman's term), and the chapter (pp. 600 to 631) never uses it, nor does NCERT for this expression. As an MCQ key ((c) on p. 648, (a) in Q11) it is non-NCERT and arguably backwards. Correction: "(1 − N/K) is the fraction of K still unused; its fall towards 0 reflects rising environmental resistance." Confidence medium-high. Same wording in 5 places.
2. p. 653 Q7: "Assertion (A): Very small animals are rarely found in polar regions." False as stated: small ectotherms (springtails, mites, nematodes, tardigrades, insects) are abundant in polar regions; the solution itself says "small endotherms". Correction: "Very small endotherms ...". Confidence high.
3. p. 644 Stage 4 row, bullet "Contracting pyramid ... Japan, Germany" and p. 643 table Stage 4 "Low (~12-15) | Low (~8-10) ... Developed nations (Germany, Japan)": Japan and Germany have birth rates below death rates (about 7 to 9 and 11 to 12 per 1000), not 12 to 15 over 8 to 10; they are in natural decrease, which the classic four-stage model does not cover (a fifth stage), and Stage 4 is described as "near zero or slight growth". Also the table gives no unit for the rates (per 1000 population per year). Confidence medium-high. Example E.1's Country B (12 and 10) fits the table but not Japan or Germany.
4. p. 644 vs p. 645: working-age group is "15-64 years" in the method but "15-60 years" in Example E.2 (and "above 60"). Inconsistent. Confidence high.
5. p. 644 "the total fertility rate (TFR) fell below replacement level (2.1) in several states well before 2010 and nationally by about 2020" and "demographic dividend (roughly 2005-2050)", "India passed 1 billion in about 2000", "India 1950s-1970s Stage 2, 1990s-2010s Stage 3": national TFR below 2.1 is reported for 2019 to 2021 (NFHS-5: 2.0), 1 billion about 2000 (census 2001: 1.028 billion), the dividend window is usually quoted as about 2005 to 2055; stages by decade are a simplification. No source given in the book. Confidence medium (low-medium for the window). 
6. p. 645 Practice E4 / p. 646 answer 4: "Why do developed countries with very low birth rates (below replacement) not experience rapid population decline? Because of population momentum". Momentum is a young age structure delaying decline; most developed countries have older structures, and the slow decline is mainly long life expectancy, past large cohorts and immigration. Confidence medium.
7. p. 644 Example E.1: "Country A: High birth rate and falling death rate = Stage 2": the data give only a death rate of 12 per 1000 (already low), so "falling" is not in the data; natural increase 38 − 12 = 26 per 1000 (2.6 % a year); Country B 12 − 10 = 2 per 1000 (0.2 %). Answer stands. Confidence low.
8. p. 650 statement (iii) "UV rays are not harmful to many organisms" keyed False: as worded it is arguably true (many organisms are unharmed); the key explains "UV-B causes sunburn and DNA damage; UV-C is mutagenic": UV-C is absorbed by the atmosphere and does not reach the ground, and UV-B is also mutagenic. Statement (i) "Light is essential for life on Earth" is false for deep-sea chemosynthetic communities (overgeneral, "NCERT" tag attached to (ii) only). Key "4 statements are correct" depends on both. Confidence medium-high for (iii) wording, medium for (i).
9. p. 648 Archetype 3: the trap warns "R is a consequence of A, not its cause" yet the example (R: "the birth rate and the death rate become equal", a restatement of A) is keyed (a). Confidence low-medium.
10. p. 653 Q8 "excludes Chthamalus from the lower intertidal zone" versus Addendum C answer 5 (p. 641) "excluded from the mid-shore": Connell's Chthamalus lives on the upper shore and Balanus on the lower; the book's two statements use different zones. Confidence low.
11. p. 655 Q12: "Cuscuta draws all nutrition ... it no longer needs to photosynthesise" overgeneral: some Cuscuta species retain traces of chlorophyll and photosynthesise a little (NCERT: "lost its chlorophyll and leaves"). Key point 2 lists "sea anemone-clownfish (commensalism)": NCERT's example, but the pair is usually described as mutualism. Confidence low-medium.
