## Additional topic C
- Kalam: "Complete competitors for the same limited resource cannot coexist indefinitely."
- Introduction: "Topic 04 states Gause's competitive exclusion principle, competitive release and resource partitioning, and names the barnacle case (Balanus and Chthamalus). It does not develop how the two barnacle species are separated along the shore, why one excludes the other at each tidal height, what Connell's removal experiment showed, or the ways species partition resources (such as MacArthur's warblers). This additional topic fills that gap." ("perennial favourite" cut).
- Method: "Complete competitors, species with identical niches that compete for the same limiting resource in a stable environment, cannot coexist indefinitely; the better competitor excludes the other from the area. NCERT states it for closely related species and only where resources are limiting."
