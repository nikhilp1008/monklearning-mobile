Where the book is loose or wrong I wrote the safe version, and the header lists each case. The main ones:
- **Exam claims:** no exam-share superlatives, shares or years. The F point calls the distribution the book's estimate and gives no shares.
- **Salinity:** given as inland water below 5 ppt, not "freshwater". The 45 to 50 °C thermal-springs anchor is dropped.
- **Regulators and conformers:** birds and mammals are "the usual regulators", not "all". "About 99 per cent of animals" is attributed to the chapter.
- **Blubber:** I put it with the structural (morphological) group, because the cheat sheet wrongly lists it as physiological.
- **Allen's rule and altitude:** Allen's rule is a tendency. Altitude acclimatisation is "mainly more red blood cells".
- **Population symbols:** N is a count and density is N over S, so the book's D for density and N<sub>t</sub> as density are avoided.
- **Growth numbers:** K is not "the maximum ever reached". Growth is greatest at K/2. 880, 443, 37.5 and 50 individuals per year are recomputed and agree.
- **Interactions:** the clownfish and sea anemone pair is flagged as usually treated as mutualism. Competition is "the weaker is hurt more". The yucca moth is written as the book prints it (Pronuba).
- **Gause's principle:** stated for complete competitors, with the three conditions.
- **Paine's starfish study:** no species count, since the book's "more than ten" is doubtful.
- **Additional topic A:** 0.559 is rounded to 0.56.
- **Allee effect:** the signs of the Allee equation are checked.
- **Functional responses:** the examples are given as illustrations, not rules.
- **Human population:** the book's India dates are labelled approximate. The 15 to 64 against 15 to 60 working-age inconsistency is noted. The "momentum explains developed countries" claim is dropped.
