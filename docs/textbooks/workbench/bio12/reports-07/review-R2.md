Scope: Additional topics A to H in content/textbooks/bio-12-07/topic-4-extras-a-d.ts (A to D, with the shared introduction) and extras-e-h.ts (E to H), and key points with topic index 4 to 11 in lib/revise/keypoints-biology-12-07.ts. Book pp. 379 to 407. Nothing edited.

Method: every Additional topic was compared with the book text (bio12.paged.txt, pp. 379 to 407) by an automatic word and 4-gram diff plus a word-count (bag) diff, and the table pages (383, 393, 400, 406) were read on the image. Flags below are therefore almost all errors in the book (source: book) except where marked.

## Flags, most serious first

### 1. Additional G, CPR and DRABC: pulse check, no call for help (unsafe, source: book, certain on current guidance)
Where: G Method, DRABC list and "3. CPR" paragraph (p. 395, p. 396); Example G.3 steps and ans (p. 397); Practice G.2 answer.
Says now: "C (Circulation): Check for pulse; begin CPR if no pulse." / "When a victim has no pulse and no breathing: 30 chest compressions ... Continue until pulse returns or medical help arrives." / G.3 ans "No pulse and no breathing; 30 compressions ∶ 2 breaths." / Practice 2 "C: check circulation (pulse) and initiate CPR if absent."
Wrong: current Indian Resuscitation Council, AHA and ERC teaching: a lay rescuer starts compressions on a person who is unresponsive and not breathing normally (an occasional gasp does not count) without a pulse check (unreliable), and calls for emergency help first (112 in India). DRABC has no "call for help" step. "Until pulse returns" cannot be judged by a lay rescuer.
Fix [NEW]: DRABC last line: "C (Circulation): Look for signs of life. If the person is unresponsive and not breathing normally, call for emergency help (112 in India) and begin CPR at once; a lay rescuer does not need to check for a pulse."
CPR paragraph [NEW]: "When a victim is unresponsive and not breathing normally (an occasional gasp does not count): call for emergency help, then give 30 chest compressions (lower half of sternum, 5–6 cm deep, 100–120 per minute) followed by 2 rescue breaths. Continue until the person starts to breathe normally, medical help arrives or you can no longer continue."
G.3 steps first sentence [NEW]: "CPR is initiated when a victim is unresponsive and not breathing normally." G.3 ans [NEW]: "Unresponsive and not breathing normally; 30 compressions ∶ 2 breaths." Practice 2 answer end [NEW]: "C: look for signs of life and start CPR if the person is unresponsive and not breathing normally (after calling for help)."
Exam note [NEW, optional]: "A question that says 'no pulse and no breathing' expects CPR."
Propagation: key point G1 (topic 10) already states the safe rule, so chapter text and key point now disagree until this is fixed.
