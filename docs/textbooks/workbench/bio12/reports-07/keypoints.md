**Where I stated the safe version instead of the book's claim** (the header comment lists all of these)
- **Sickle-cell and malaria:** the protection belongs to carriers of the sickle-cell trait, not to people with sickle-cell anaemia.
- **Vectors and cold:** the vectors are named as female *Anopheles*, *Culex* and *Aedes*, with the housefly kept as a mechanical carrier of amoebiasis. The common cold "does not reach the lungs".
- **Plasmodium order:** I used the corrected order A mosquito, B human, C human, D mosquito, back to A. I did not quote Figure 7.1 stage numbers, because the text's six steps and the figure's stages split steps 5 and 6 differently.
- **Immunity and cancer:** graft rejection is "mainly T-cell". Malignant spread is by blood and lymph. The HIV delay is "several years, about 5 to 10 in the chapter". Anti-tetanus antibodies are passive and a tetanus vaccine is active. Benzopyrene is "a carcinogen in smoke".
- **Drugs:** amphetamines are stimulants, not sedatives. Heroin is made from morphine. The addiction/dependence split is given with a note that dependence has a mental side too. The ethanol percentages are dropped.
- **Hypersensitivity:** the "always" rules are labelled rules of thumb about question wording.
- **Autoimmunity:** *S. pyogenes* causes the throat infection and rheumatic fever follows it. Rheumatoid arthritis is not given as purely Type III.
- **Immunodeficiency:** malnutrition is "a major cause", not "the single largest".
- **Cancer staging:** the grouping differs between cancers, T2 is above 2 cm and up to 5 cm, and a marker does not prove cancer alone.
- **STIs:** chlamydia is "a leading" cause of tubal infertility, and the 70% and 15 to 40% figures are dropped. The spirochete is seen by dark-field microscopy and does not stain well by Gram stain. NAAT is named for both gonorrhoea and chlamydia.

The bactericidal/bacteriostatic split is a general pattern, with streptomycin as a bactericidal ribosome drug. I dropped "permanently resistant" and "within days". *Penicillium notatum* is the book's name, with a note that it is now *P. rubens*.
