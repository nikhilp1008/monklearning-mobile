5. Left as the book has it, by decision: kalam C and D, the F key text for D, the G "three most common traps" sentence (it carries the estimate label), the U6 "catalytic component" answer.
6. Numbers with NBSP before units (4 kb, 7 kb) are kept as they were; the new sentences use ordinary spaces.
