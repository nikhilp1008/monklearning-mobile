## Suspected book errors (page, quote, why, correction, confidence)
1. p. 46/47 Method bullet "Wing (samara): flat, light extension of the fruit wall; found in Acer and Shorea (dipterocarp)". In Shorea (and other dipterocarps) the wings are enlarged calyx lobes, not the fruit wall; a samara proper is a winged fruit. Correct: "found in Acer; in Shorea the wings are enlarged persistent sepals". Confidence high.
2. p. 47 "The fruit (not the seed alone) is winged in many anemochorous species": fine; but the table row "Anemochory ... Hypochaeris (cat's ear): pappus" fine. "Zoochory ... Bidens" fine.
3. p. 59 Q1 and its answer: "no enzyme degrades it" (also Part I p. 10 per brief): overgeneral. NCERT: no enzyme that degrades sporopollenin is known so far. Correct "no enzyme known so far degrades it". Confidence high.
4. p. 58 Traps "Cleistogamous = never opens (always autogamous)" and p. 60 Q9 (iii) "Xenogamy is the only pollination type that brings genetically different pollen" (both overgeneral/absolute; the key (a) depends on (iii) being true). In practice cleistogamous flowers are autogamous; xenogamy is the only one that brings genetically different pollen in the standard NCERT scheme. Correct with "in the NCERT scheme" and "normally". Confidence medium.
5. p. 57 Archetype 6 rule: "anything formed from a gamete or products of meiosis = n": the zygote and endosperm are formed from gametes and are 2n and 3n; the rule's own next clause handles endosperm, but the sentence as a general ladder is wrong. Correct: "anything that is a product of meiosis (spore or gamete) = n". Confidence medium. Also "anything formed before the last meiosis = 2n" is loose (PMC, MMC, zygote, endosperm...).
6. p. 54 Distribution: counts 16 + 13 + 12 + 5 + 22 = 68; shares 24 + 19 + 18 + 7 + 32 = 100; each share = count/68 rounded (23.5, 19.1, 17.6, 7.4, 32.4): all correct.
