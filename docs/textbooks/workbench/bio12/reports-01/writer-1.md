## Suspected book errors / overgeneral claims (page, quote, note)
- p. 9 "This is the single most heavily-tested block of the chapter"; "near-certain" (pp. 9, 23); p. 23 "A guaranteed scoring zone"; p. 16 "a perennial CBSE 2–3 mark question": superlatives, unverifiable.
- p. 10 exine "is degraded by no enzyme" (also p. 13 Ex 4 R): overgeneral; sporopollenin is extremely resistant but "very resistant to enzymes" is safer.
- p. 10 xenogamy "the only type bringing genetically different pollen": overgeneral (geitonogamy within a heterozygous...no; but also autogamy never; ok), keep "the type bringing genetically different pollen"; also papaya/maize examples: maize is monoecious, also geitonogamous.
- p. 10 cleistogamy "always autogamous": NCERT says "invariably"; acceptable, low confidence.
- p. 11 and p. 13 pollen tube "reaches the ovule through the micropyle": chalazogamy exists (Casuarina), medium confidence.
- p. 11 Ex dioecy/monoecy logic is correct; "monoecious unisexual plant prevents only autogamy" correct.
- p. 17 "Pronuba yuccasella": current name Tegeticula yuccasella; "the moth is the only creature that pollinates the yucca ... Remove one and the other goes extinct": overgeneral (several Yucca/moth species, some Yucca have other pollinators); medium.
- p. 18 "tallest flower ... Amorphophallus": it is an inflorescence (book says so); fine.
- p. 20 pitfall "the single most common artificial-hybridisation trap": superlative.
- p. 23 "found only in flowering plants. No other group on Earth does this" and "Unique to angiosperms": Ephedra and Gnetum show double fertilisation (second fusion gives a supernumerary embryo, not endosperm). Medium-high.
- p. 17 "Siphonogamy ... the angiosperm mode": conifers are also siphonogamous. Medium.
- p. 24 "synergids and antipodal cells degenerate after fertilization": antipodals can persist or proliferate in some species (grasses); overgeneral, low.
- p. 25 "Endosperm develops before the embryo": typical, not universal. "ruminate endosperm, e.g. Rubiaceae": usual examples are Annonaceae, Myristicaceae, palms; low confidence.
- p. 25 "the oldest viable seeds recovered (lupine ...)" superlative: Silene stenophylla (about 30,000 years, regenerated from fruit tissue) is older; NCERT lupine about 10,000 years.
- p. 25 and p. 27 box "parthenogenesis = an animal term": parthenogenesis also occurs in plants (apomixis, algae); overgeneral. Topic 04 reuses this claim.
- p. 14 Ex 3 S-I wording fine. p. 12 "Pollen grain: a partly-germinated microspore" acceptable.
