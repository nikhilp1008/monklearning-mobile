## Suspected book errors (page, quote, why, correction, confidence)
1. p. 222 "Each is a regrouping of the sixteen dihybrid boxes." after listing "1 : 2 : 1 (incomplete dominance, codominance)": 1 ∶ 2 ∶ 1 is a monohybrid ratio (four boxes), not a regrouping of sixteen. Correct: say "each of the dihybrid ratios 9 ∶ 3 ∶ 3 ∶ 1 ... 15 ∶ 1 is a regrouping of the sixteen boxes". High.
2. p. 216 Archetype 6 "Two unaffected parents → an affected child → recessive trait (both parents must be carriers)": true for autosomal recessive only; in X-linked recessive the father is not a carrier. Correct: "(for an autosomal trait both parents are carriers)". High.
3. p. 213 Archetype 1 "Anything test-crossed with homozygous recessive → ... each gamete type gives one phenotypic class in equal proportion": equal only for unlinked genes; with linkage (Archetype 3 and Additional F) the classes are unequal. Correct: "in equal proportion if the genes assort independently". High.
4. p. 214 Archetype 3 "RF can never exceed 50 %; above that the genes behave as if unlinked" and p. 217 "Linkage groups = haploid chromosome number n": (a) observed two-point RF is capped at 50 %, but map distances (sums of intervals) can exceed 50 cM; (b) for a species with heteromorphic sex chromosomes the number of linkage groups is n + 1 (human male: 22 + X + Y = 24). Also the trap's "The textbook defines linkage groups as 'all genes on one chromosome'". Correct with "in a test cross, the observed RF between two genes is at most 50 %" and "n (n + 1 where X and Y are mapped separately)". Medium.
5. p. 206 to 208 Additional D, "all children of an affected mother are affected; no child of an affected father is affected", table "Skips generation: Never", practice 3 "All three (100%)", D.2 and D.1: heteroplasmy and the threshold effect mean an affected mother may have unaffected children and a trait can skip a generation; rare paternal mtDNA transmission has been reported. D.1 itself invokes heteroplasmy and then says "In strict maternal inheritance, no child ... of an unaffected mother can be affected": the example contradicts its own table ("never") unless "pure homoplasmic" is stated. The text also says both "all mitochondria in the zygote come from the egg" and "the sperm contributes virtually no mitochondria". Correct: "usually", "almost always", add heteroplasmy. Medium-high.
6. p. 217 "Sickle-cell is the single most mis-classified mutation": unsupported superlative (see exception 6). Medium. p. 212 "striking regularity", "appear in almost every paper", p. 214 "regular NEET distractor" (p. 206): unsupported frequency claims, labelled once.
7. p. 215 "Linkage → discovered by Morgan": linkage was first reported by Bateson and Punnett (1905 to 1906); Morgan explained it and coined the terms for Drosophila. Matches NCERT, so acceptable for an exam answer; low.
8. p. 220 Q8 "GAG → GTG in the DNA": correct for the coding strand (template reads CTC to CAC; mRNA GAG to GUG); say "coding strand". Low. The problem also relies on Q1 of Topic 03.
9. p. 220 Q7: options (a) (1/2)³ and (c) 1/8 are numerically equal (both 1/8); not a defect for the key (b) but both are distractors. p. 218 Q1 "Law of Linkage" is not a Mendelian law (fine as a distractor).
10. p. 212 Distribution counts "3–4, 2–3, 2–3, 1–2, 1" total 9 to 13 questions per paper: not checkable; label added. "2n + 1" (classes for n gene pairs, same as Topic 04's pattern): fine.
11. p. 216 Archetype 6: "non-disjunction of sex chromosomes during meiosis (one X chromosome is lost)": loss of a sex chromosome in a gamete (or in early mitosis); acceptable. p. 215 "the egg always contributes X": true in humans apart from non-disjunction. Low.
12. p. 212 F practice 1 "only two of the four chromatids take part": true for one crossover; the 50 % ceiling argument is correct. Fine.
