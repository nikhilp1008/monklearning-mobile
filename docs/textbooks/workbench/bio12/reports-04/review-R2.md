Step 3 [NEW] "Children of an affected mother: usually all of them carry the mutation, both sexes alike, although how strongly each is affected can differ (heteroplasmy)." Practice 3 answer [NEW] "All three would be expected to carry the mutation (the trait is passed through the egg cytoplasm to every child regardless of sex), though the severity can vary." Kind MISL/BIO. Source B. Confidence certain.
