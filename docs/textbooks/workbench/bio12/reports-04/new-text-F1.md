## Topic 02
11. Exam Relevance box (flag 13): "A frequently tested topic, especially on linkage groups, recombination-frequency/map-unit numericals and the four sex-determination systems with their heterogametic sex." (CUET and CBSE sentences and SHARE kept.)
12. Limiting conditions (1) (flag 6, own): "the maximum observable recombination frequency between two genes in a test cross is 50% (at which they behave as if unlinked)."
13. Definitions, Complete linkage (flag 4): "no recombinants formed (rare, as in male Drosophila, where crossing over does not occur)."
14. Definitions, Linkage group (flag 3): "the genes on one chromosome, which tend to be inherited together. The number of linkage groups equals the haploid number n when the sex chromosomes are alike or counted as one pair (pea 7, Drosophila 4, maize 10); where X and Y differ and are counted as two groups there is one more (a human male has 22 + X + Y = 24, a female 23)."
15. Definitions, Coupling and repulsion (flag 10): "In coupling, the two dominant alleles lie on the same chromosome (AB/ab) and tend to stay together in a gamete; in repulsion, each chromosome carries one dominant and one recessive allele (Ab/aB), so the two dominant alleles come from different parents and tend to stay apart."
16. Procedure B limiting condition (flag 4): "The ceiling is 50%: at 50% the genes behave as if they were on different chromosomes."
17. Procedure C step 3 (flag 7): "Sanity check: for short distances the two shorter distances add up to the outer distance (over long stretches double crossovers make the measured values fall short)." Why line: "... the two short segments sum to the long one (for short distances)."
18. Procedure F (flag 3, own wording for the qualifier): "... equals the haploid chromosome number n (when the sex chromosomes are alike or counted as one pair), not the diploid number 2n."
19. Worked example 2 trap, last sentence (flag 3): "Halve 2n to the haploid number (the chapter’s rule for a plant with no distinct sex chromosomes)."
20. Pitfall 1 (flag 13): "A very common slip." Pitfall 4 (flag 6): "Forgetting the 50% recombination ceiling. The recombination frequency between two genes in a test cross cannot exceed 50% (a crossover involves only two of the four chromatids); genes showing 50% behave as if unlinked. A “60% recombination” option for two genes is wrong. (Long map distances, built by adding short intervals, can exceed 50 cM.)"
21. Cheat sheet (flags 3, 6, 15): linkage groups "human 23 (24 in a male, since X and Y differ)"; RF "(ceiling 50% for two genes in a test cross)" (own); sex determination "temperature-dependent (crocodile; a fifth type, not in the table above). In Drosophila sex depends on the ratio of X chromosomes to sets of autosomes, not on the Y alone."
