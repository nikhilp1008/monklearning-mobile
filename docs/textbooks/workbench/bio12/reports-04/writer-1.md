## Suspected errors and overgeneral lines (copied as printed; page, quote, why, correction, confidence)
1. p. 163 "This is the single most question-dense subtopic of the chapter. NEET reliably draws 2–3 questions every year": unsupported superlative and frequency. Correct to "a heavily tested topic"; labelled once. High.
2. p. 164 "Recessive allele: expresses only in the homozygous state": X-linked recessive alleles also express in the hemizygous male (the book's own p. 173), and the 'only' fails with incomplete dominance. Add "(or hemizygous)". High.
3. p. 164, p. 165 "Law of Dominance: in a heterozygote, only the dominant allele expresses": true only under complete dominance (the book's own incomplete dominance and codominance, p. 166). Add "(complete dominance)". High.
4. p. 166 "The mechanism: the recessive allele produces a non-functional (or reduced) enzyme" stated for incomplete dominance in general; it is the usual pigment-gene explanation, not general. Say "in a pigment gene such as this". Medium.
5. p. 169 Pro-Tip "never draw a giant Punnett square" and p. 165 "Gamete types = 2^n": hold only for independent assortment, no linkage (Topic 02 is the exception). Add the condition. Medium.
6. p. 170 and p. 177 Exam Relevance: "A NEET favourite — expect 2–3 questions yearly", "A high-yield, high-frequency subtopic ... almost every year", "CBSE Boards love this": frequency claims and a superlative; labelled. High.
7. p. 171 "Linkage group: all the genes on a single chromosome, inherited as a unit" and "(Complete linkage) genes never separate": linkage is rarely complete (crossing over, the book's own incomplete linkage). Replace "inherited as a unit" with "tending to be inherited together". High.
8. p. 171, p. 176 "(linkage groups = haploid number) ... human 23" and p. 173 Procedure F, p. 174 Example 2 "Always halve": a species with heteromorphic sex chromosomes has n + 1 linkage groups (human male: 22 autosomes + X + Y = 24); 23 is the haploid number only. Add "autosomal and equal-sized sex chromosomes". Medium-high.
9. p. 172 "The four sex-determination systems" table has four, but the p. 176 cheat sheet lists five (adds "temperature-dependent (crocodile)", not in Section 2). Internal count mismatch; also Drosophila sex is set by the X-to-autosome ratio, not the Y (XO flies are sterile males). Medium.
10. p. 173 Procedure C "Sanity check: middle-gene distances should add up to the outer distance": additive only for short distances (double crossovers, interference); the book qualifies it only in p. 176's Pro-Tip. Add the condition here. High.
11. p. 175 and p. 176 "ceiling 50%", "Recombination frequency can never exceed 50% ... A '60% recombination' option is always wrong": right for recombination frequency between two loci in one cross, but p. 172 equates RF with map distance (cM), and map distances built by adding short intervals can exceed 50 cM (whole chromosomes are hundreds of cM). Say "recombination frequency between two genes". Medium-high.
12. p. 176 "The single most common slip in this subtopic": unsupported superlative. High.
13. p. 177 "The catch is that most [Mendelian disorders] are recessive": many (Huntington, Marfan, achondroplasia) are dominant; "many are recessive". Medium.
14. p. 180 "the 6th codon changes from GAG to GUG": that is the mRNA codon (gene DNA is GAG to GTG); NCERT prints it the same way. Low.
15. p. 181 "This single distinction is the most commonly tested contrast in the whole subtopic": unsupported superlative. High.
16. p. 183 Pitfall 2 "When the father is normal, no daughter can be colour blind or haemophilic ... An affected daughter requires an affected father" (also p. 184 cheat sheet): true barring a new mutation, X-chromosome loss or skewed inactivation; and she also needs a carrier or affected mother. Medium-low.
17. p. 183 Pro-Tip "sons' risk = mother's carrier status": loose; a carrier mother gives an affected son 1/2 of the time. Say "a son's risk is 1/2 if the mother is a carrier". Medium.
18. p. 182 "the highest you can get without an affected mother": true only among the crosses with a carrier or normal mother; low.
19. p. 164 "Mendel ... for seven patient years (1856–1863)": the span is eight calendar years; NCERT says seven years of crosses; low.
"always" and "never" lines checked: p. 173 "the mother (XX) always gives an X" and "the father determines" (right, the book gives the ZW exception in the same step); p. 164 "never blend inside a gamete" (right); p. 176 see 11.
