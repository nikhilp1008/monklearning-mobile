## Suspected book errors / issues (page, quote, why, correction, confidence)
- p. 590 "NEET returns to Chapter 10 with striking selectivity", p. 591 "asked just as regularly", p. 592 "highest-yielding hard question shape": unsupported exam superlatives; labelled; confidence high they are unsourced.
- p. 591 Archetype 1: "GEAC stands for ... (c) Genetic Engineering Approval Committee" (also Q2 p. 595, key p. 597). NCERT (Class 12) uses "Approval"; the body's official name is Genetic Engineering Appraisal Committee. Copy NCERT; add the note. Confidence medium-high.
- p. 593 Archetype 4: "(iii) Incorrect: cry protein forms during sporulation, not 'any phase'". NCERT only says the crystals form "during a particular phase of their growth"; "sporulation" is true microbiology but not NCERT wording. Low-medium.
- p. 593 to 594 Archetype 6: the answer "(a)" refers to options the book does not print; and its labels (A = Insulin, B = free C-peptide) differ from Q10's labels (A = free C-peptide, B = insulin) on p. 596 (both internally consistent). Noted, not an error.
- p. 578 table and p. 577: "Germ-line transmission: Variable (~10-30%)" repeats the proportion of offspring carrying the transgene (p. 578 "approximately 10-30% of offspring typically carry the transgene"); that is integration/founder efficiency, not germ-line transmission. Medium.
- p. 577 "About 95% of transgenic animals are mice" (NCERT: "more than 95 per cent"); p. 579 "approximately 95%". Keep, medium.
- p. 578 and p. 579 Ex B.1, answer 5 (p. 580): "Rosie ... produced by microinjection of the human α-lactalbumin gene into the pronucleus of a fertilised cow embryo". NCERT gives no method; Rosie (1997) is NCERT's; the method is not supported in the book. Low-medium; the book's year 1997 kept.
- p. 596 Q9 (iv) and key p. 598: "α-1 antitrypsin produced in transgenic sheep". NCERT names the protein, not the animal; transgenic sheep is real but is the book's addition. Low.
- p. 586 Q2 (ii) answer "these retroviral vectors need dividing cells"; the answer to Q6 (p. 586) says retroviral vectors "including lentiviral vectors" integrate; lentiviral vectors can transduce non-dividing cells. The "these" in the answer saves it; noted. Low.
- p. 581 PCR "Typical time: 1-2 minutes per kilobase": about 1 minute per kb for Taq; ok as a range. p. 582 Ex C.3 "starting from one DNA molecule ... 2^25 copies": theoretical (the first cycles give longer-than-target products); standard textbook simplification. Low.
- p. 584 table: retrovirus "Immunogenicity: Low" and the vector comparison are not NCERT; plausible. p. 584 "Used experimentally ... CFTR via inhalation": fine.
- p. 587 turmeric: US Patent 5,401,504, filed 1993, revoked 1997 (the patent was granted in 1995; "Filed in 1993" is correct); neem EP 436257 granted 1994 and revoked 2005; Basmati US 5,663,484, 1997 (several claims later withdrawn or narrowed). I recall these as correct. Nagoya Protocol adopted 2010, in force 2014, India a Party: correct. "Access only with prior informed consent" (p. 588) simplifies: the Protocol also requires mutually agreed terms. "When granted patent applications citing" (p. 587) is a wording slip (copied). No new dates added. Confidence medium-high these are right.
- p. 594 trap: "Bt cotton is the only Bt crop commercially grown in India" and "Bt brinjal was not approved for commercial cultivation": true to my knowledge (2010 moratorium) but not NCERT; unqualified "only". Medium.
- Overgeneral wording, copied: p. 594 "They never meet" (two insulin routes); p. 580 "always produces 100% transgenic offspring" is a student's wrong claim (intentional); p. 595 "All Bt toxins kill all pests" is a trap statement (intentional).
- Errata (1): the Bt cotton spectrum is lepidopteran bollworms only on p. 595 ("cotton bollworms (lepidopterans)"); no "dipterans, coleopterans" claim appears on pp. 577 to 599. The errata (2) pieces (production tags, stray label) are not on my pages.
