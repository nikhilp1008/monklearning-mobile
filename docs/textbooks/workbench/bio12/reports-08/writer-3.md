## Suspected book errors (page; quote; correction; confidence)
1. p.463/469 and p.478 BOD: "oxidise all organic matter in 1 litre of water over 5 days at 20°C" (also F, Key Distinctions 3 "all the organic matter"). BOD5 measures only part (about 60-70 per cent of carbonaceous demand); "all" and "5 days" together contradict. Keep NCERT wording, add: standard test is a 5-day, 20°C measure. High.
2. p.455/457 and p.457 A.3 "No gas is released" / "No CO2 is released in lactic acid fermentation": true of homolactic fermentation only; heterofermentative lactobacilli produce CO2 and ethanol. Add "homolactic". High.
3. p.466 table and E.2 (p.467): Rhizobium "Obligate symbiont": rhizobia live free in soil and fix N2 only in nodules; "facultative symbiont, fixes nitrogen only in nodules". Med-high.
4. p.470 Archetype 1 "the yeast Monascus purpureus" (NCERT wording, kept): Monascus is a filamentous fungus (mould), not a yeast; p.478 calls it "fungus". Med-high.
5. p.475 Q3 / p.471 / p.478: cyclosporin A from Trichoderma polysporum is the NCERT attribution; current literature: Tolypocladium inflatum. High (note, keep NCERT).
6. p.474 Traps: "Anabaena azollae" is the older name (current Trichormus/Nostoc azollae). Med.
7. p.476 Q6 and p.477 E.1: "Bt ... its spores contain a protoxin"; the protoxin is in the parasporal crystal beside the spore. Med-high.
8. p.461 Standard Values: "Drinking water standard: Below 5 mg/L" BOD; no such drinking-water BOD standard (CPCB class A source water is 2 mg/L or less). Med.
9. p.458 table: "Streptomycin Broad-spectrum" and "Moderate-spectrum" category for erythromycin; classes vary by source, not NCERT. Low-med. B intro defines narrow as "either Gram-positive or Gram-negative": a simplification.
10. p.468 E.5 answer: "naturally mycorrhizal-dependent (maize, onion, most vegetables)": Brassicas (cabbage, used in E.1) and spinach are non-mycorrhizal. Med-high.
11. p.478 Q10(b): Bt "leaves non-target insects ... unharmed"; Bt kills non-target Lepidoptera; and "no toxic residues" is absolute. Med.
12. p.474 Trap: "Fungi = phosphorus ... bacteria and cyanobacteria = nitrogen": mycorrhizae also supply water and other nutrients (the book says so on p.467); simplification. Low-med.
