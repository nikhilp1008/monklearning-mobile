## Suspected book errors and overgeneral claims (page, quote, note, confidence)
Dates and numbers

- p. 299 "The first non-cellular life is thought to have been RNA (~3 bya)": NCERT gives about 3 billion years for the first non-cellular forms and about 2000 mya for cellular life; "RNA world" is a hypothesis, not NCERT text. Copied. Medium.
- p. 298 (also pp. 301, 303 MCQ 3, 304) "Human–chimp DNA differs in only ~1.8% of bases": usual figures are about 1.2% for aligned single-base differences and about 4 to 5% counting insertions and deletions; 1.8% is not an NCERT number. Copied. Low to medium.
- p. 301 "cytochrome-C ... essentially identical" (human and chimp): identical sequence; fine. p. 301 "Continental drift (Wegener, 1912)": fine.
- p. 307 and p. 314 "Modern Synthetic Theory (1942)", "Modern Synthesis (Huxley, 1942)": 1942 is the date of Huxley's book; the synthesis is usually placed in the 1930s and 1940s (Dobzhansky 1937). Copied. Medium.
- p. 298 "Concept developed by H.F. Osborn (early 1900s)" (adaptive radiation, 1902 usually): not an NCERT attribution; fine. Low.
- p. 297 Panspermia "early Greek thinkers": the usual name is Anaxagoras (and Arrhenius, 1903, for spores); NCERT does not name one. Low.
