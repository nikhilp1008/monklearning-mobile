## Suspected book errors and overgeneral claims (page, quote, note; confidence)
- p. 489 "Read the top strand left-to-right (GAATTC) and the bottom strand left-to-right (also GAATTC reading 5′→3′)": the bottom strand is printed 3′–CTTAAG–5′; read left to right it is CTTAAG; GAATTC is read right to left. Should say right-to-left. High.
- p. 481 and p. 486 Cohen and Boyer 1972 and a plasmid of Salmonella typhimurium (also pp. 484, 485): this is NCERT's wording; the 1972 to 1973 Cohen-Boyer work used plasmids (pSC101 and others) of other origins and the paper is 1973. Keep, low-medium confidence that the exact source and year are looser than stated.
- p. 481 Nobel 1978 for Arber, Nathans, Smith: correct (Physiology or Medicine). "first one discovered was Hind II" (pp. 488, 494, 495): consistent with NCERT, fine.
- p. 484 A-R Q2 "introduction of only the desirable gene" and p. 485 "Precision is the whole point": real vectors also carry markers and origins; "only" overgeneral (NCERT: "only the desirable gene" similar). Low.
- p. 484 practice 5 and p. 480 "asexual reproduction ... introduces no new useful variation": mutation introduces variation in asexual lines; overgeneral. Low-medium.
- p. 489 "Blunt ends can be joined ... without sequence specificity": blunt ends can be ligated only in the sense of any blunt end to any; fine. "Disarmed pathogens (Ti plasmid, retroviruses)" p. 491: a plasmid is not a pathogen, it is from one. Low.
- p. 489 pBR322 "several unique restriction sites (EcoRI, HindIII, BamHI, SalI, PvuII, PstI, ClaI)": PvuII sits in the rop region (in neither resistance gene) and the figure caption names PvuI as well; fine. No worked example uses PstI or PvuI (insert in ampR gives amp-sensitive, tet-resistant); only BamHI is worked, recomputed correct: recombinant is amp-resistant, tet-sensitive.
- p. 494 practice 5 answer "the vector is destroyed": a circular vector with two sites gives two fragments (n sites, n fragments), one of which keeps ori; "destroyed" is loose. Low-medium.
- p. 496 "The separation depends only on size", p. 501 A-R "strictly according to size" and "sole sorting variable": conformation (supercoiled vs linear), gel concentration and voltage also matter; "only/strictly/sole" overgeneral. Medium. p. 496 "pure, unstained DNA cannot be seen directly even under UV": DNA absorbs UV (shadowing), so not strictly true. Low.
- p. 502 pitfall "Stating 'positive' or 'cathode' is the most common error here": unsupported superlative. p. 487 "A perennial scoring zone", p. 480 "high-certainty marks", p. 496 "asked almost every year": unverified frequency claims.
- p. 502 MCQ 2 option (a) "acrylamide only": polyacrylamide gels are also used for DNA, so the option is wrong only through "only". Low.
- p. 495 pitfall and p. 494 MCQ 1 explanation "EcoRI is the most commonly illustrated enzyme": fine as an exam remark.
