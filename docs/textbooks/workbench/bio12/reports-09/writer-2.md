## Suspected book errors (page, quote, why, confidence)
- p. 507 "so only cells carrying the recombinant DNA survive": antibiotic selection keeps cells with the plasmid, recombinant or not (Topic 02 p. 491 says so; p. 516 says "only plasmid-bearing cells survived"). Contradiction; replace with "cells carrying the vector". High.
- p. 507 / p. 510 "EcoRI will only join a vector also cut by EcoRI"; "must be cut with the same enzyme" (cheat sheet, practice 5): overgeneral; enzymes with compatible cohesive ends (BamHI and BglII) and blunt ends also ligate. Keep NCERT's rule, add "(or an enzyme with a compatible end)". High.
- p. 507 "vectorless / direct physical methods: ... or disarmed pathogens": disarmed Ti or retroviral vectors are vectors, not vectorless. Medium.
- p. 510 pitfall "Only cellular replication uses RNA primers": overgeneral (reverse transcription, other primed systems). Medium.
- p. 511 / p. 503 "introns + no splicing machinery ... intended answer": true for genomic DNA; cDNA avoids it (A's own table note). Low.
- p. 516 "CaCl2/heat shock (which works only for bacteria)" vs p. 517 "works mainly for bacteria": inconsistent "only". Electroporation "Used for bacteria, yeast, and plant protoplasts" omits animal cells (widely used). Medium. Field strength "about 12-15 kV/cm" typical range is about 12.5-18; low.
- p. 516 biolistics "cereals and monocots that are not susceptible to Agrobacterium"; p. 518 B.4 "many cereals (maize, wheat, rice) are not natural hosts"... "suitable for any plant species"; p. 519 practice 3 "works for any plant species": overgeneral. Agrobacterium transforms rice, maize and wheat routinely (with acetosyringone); "any species" overstated. Medium-high.
- p. 516 "antibiotic resistance is selection; lacZ disruption is screening" while Topic 02 and 04 call insertional inactivation and the markers "selecting recombinants", and NCERT calls blue-white a selectable marker: terminology conflict, follow NCERT for CBSE answers. Medium.
- p. 519 practice 5 "five positive results prove ... recombinant plasmid": colony PCR shows the insert sequence is present, not that it is correct or in frame; B.3 says "confirms". Low.
- p. 513 YAC "500 kb - 1.5 Mb" yet A.1 says a YAC could carry 200 kb: 200 kb is below the table's YAC range (real YACs about 0.1-2 Mb). Low. Adenoviral "8 kb" is first-generation only. Low. Retroviral "delivers the gene permanently" ignores silencing and insertional mutagenesis. Low.
- p. 515 / p. 519: A practice 3 answer (a) "T-DNA region" vs B practice 4 answer (b) "T-DNA border sequences" for transfer: same question, different answers. Low.
- Superlatives/overgeneral left as printed: p. 503 "single most question-dense section", "hammer ... almost every year"; p. 510 "single most common loss of marks"; p. 511 "the highest number is always denaturation" (fine); p. 512 "closes every gap".
