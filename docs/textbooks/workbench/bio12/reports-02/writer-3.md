## Suspected book errors (page; text; why; fix; confidence)
1. p. 109 Q4 and its solution: "Relaxin ... does not appear outside pregnancy" and (p. 83, Topic 03) "hCG, hPL and relaxin are produced only during pregnancy": relaxin is also made by the corpus luteum in the luteal phase of the cycle (and in men). Fix: say "relaxin rises in late pregnancy" or ask "hCG". Confidence about 80%. The key (a) still the only option of the four that is pregnancy-linked.
2. p. 106 Archetype 6 and p. 111 Q9: "all muscles ... mesoderm", "Bone and all muscles" and "Lining of the digestive system: endoderm": exceptions: iris sphincter/dilator muscles and myoepithelial cells are ectodermal; the mouth and anal lining are ectodermal. Fix: "most muscles", "lining of the gut tube". Confidence 85%. Q9 key (c) still correct.
3. p. 107 "Ampulla in two places": the ampulla also occurs in the vas deferens; NCERT puts fertilisation at the ampullary-isthmic junction, not the whole "wide middle". Fix: "at least two places"; "ampullary-isthmic junction". Confidence 85%.
4. p. 104 Archetype 2: "the single most common error in this chapter" (unsupported superlative); "One sentence clears every variant". Archetype 1: "The answer is always the next station in sequence: no branching, no choice" ("always"); "opposite ends of the route" for vasa efferentia and vas deferens (they are the 3rd and 5th stations, not the ends). p. 103: "High: recurs every year", "single largest node", "Placental hormones ... appear reliably", p. 105 "Each has a predictable crack", p. 107 "deliberately engineer" and every "Seen in NEET" cell: unlabelled exam claims; label or soften.
5. p. 101 Example E.1 vs table: example says FSH is low on days 1 to 5 and peaks day 13; table (p. 100) gives menstrual-phase FSH "Rising slowly" and ovulatory-phase FSH "Surge" on day 14. Also Topic 02 gives the menstrual phase as about 3 to 5 days, E table "days 1–5" (consistent), luteal about 14 days (E: days 15–28, consistent). Fix: make the table and example agree on FSH (low then rising; peak day 13 to 14 with LH). Confidence 60%.
6. p. 100 table: GnRH "Surge" at ovulation: GnRH is pulsatile (frequency rises); "surge" is not the standard wording. Confidence 55%.
7. p. 102 E.3: "roughly 36–48 hours", "more GnRH receptors on the gonadotrophs", "never reaches this threshold" are beyond NCERT and overgeneral ("never"). The 36 to 48 h figure is a textbook estimate, not NCERT. Confidence 60% it is acceptable as labelled beyond NCERT.
8. p. 97 practice C.2: "oestrogen is required to suppress the LH surge reliably": progestogen alone can suppress the surge in some users; combined pills are more reliable. Fix "oestrogen makes suppression more reliable". Confidence 60%.
9. p. 105 Archetype 3: "Granulosa cells of a growing follicle relate to oestrogen, not progesterone": granulosa cells also become the luteal cells that secrete progesterone after ovulation; the option says "of the primary follicle" so the key stands. Confidence 55%.
10. p. 99 D.2 "reaches the ampulla": NCERT says fertilisation at the ampullary-isthmic junction. Capacitation, ZP3, acrosin, hyaluronidase, decapacitation factors and the cortical reaction are not in NCERT Class 12 text (beyond NCERT; the enzyme and ZP3 names are correct, 90%).
