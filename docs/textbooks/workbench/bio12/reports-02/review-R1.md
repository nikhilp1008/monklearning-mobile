5. Topic 03, Key processes, Fertilisation paragraph, p. 83. ## One-paragraph judgement
The chapter is accurate as copied: the transcription and every key, ratio, ploidy value and day range hold up, and the figures reproduce the book. What a teacher would correct is the book's own overstatements: the Worked Example 4 clue for W, "only in the female tract", the "only in pregnancy" hormone claims, "all muscles are mesoderm", the jumbled sperm-entry order, "opposite ends of the route", the unlabelled exam-frequency remarks and a few loose numbers (two million oocytes, 10-25 efferent tubules, "3-5 days" against "days 1-5"). The key points already fix most of these, so the chapter text should be brought into line with them.
