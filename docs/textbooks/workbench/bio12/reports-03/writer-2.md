- p. 139 LNG "renders the endometrium less receptive to implantation" and p. 140 "altering the endometrium"; contradicts p. 141 Practice 4 answer "it has no post-fertilisation effect". Current evidence (2023 labels) finds no post-fertilisation effect for LNG. Internal contradiction: certain; science: medium.
- p. 140 "a single insertion covers 5–10 years": Multiload-375 about 5 years, Cu-T 380A about 10; fine as a range. Check vs Topic 01. Low.
- p. 133 "viability ... generally placed around the start of the third trimester": usual figure is about 22-24 weeks (end of second trimester). Medium.
- p. 133 "nearly 45–50 million MTPs ... one-fifth (1/5) of all conceived pregnancies": NCERT's old figure; WHO estimates are higher (about 70 million, near 29 percent of pregnancies). Check 45-50/ (1/5) = about 225-250 million pregnancies, plausible only as NCERT's. Low-medium.
