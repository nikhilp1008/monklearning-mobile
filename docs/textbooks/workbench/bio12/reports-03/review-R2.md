### 7. C: "Exponential form (equivalent)" is not equivalent
- Where: C, Method (p. 144).
- Says now: P_t = P_0 e^{rt}, T_d = ln 2 over r, labelled "(equivalent)" to P_0 (1 + r/100)^t.
- Wrong: in e^{rt} r is a continuous rate as a decimal; in the other formula r is a yearly percentage. For r = 1.7% a year: e^{0.017 x 30} = 1.665 against 1.017^30 = 1.658. Also "T_d = 70/r comes from ln 2/r" hides that ln 2 = 0.693 (70 is a rounded 69.3 made convenient for percent rates); exact compound doubling time is ln 2 over ln(1 + r/100) (35.0 y at 2%, 41.1 y at 1.7%).
- Fix: [NEW] "Exponential form (continuous growth). P_t = P_0 e^{rt} with T_d = ln 2 over r, where r is the continuous rate as a decimal. For small r it gives nearly the same results as the yearly formula above (for 1.7% a year over 30 years, 1.665 against 1.658). Every worked example here uses the yearly formula and the rule of 70." and [NEW] after the rule of 70: "(70 is the rounded value of 100 x ln 2 = 69.3.)"
- Kind: unclear or misleading. Source: book. Confidence: certain (maths). KP topic 6 first point is correctly worded ("an approximation").

## Overall judgement
The transcription of the Additional topics is accurate and complete, and the writers' exceptions are sound. The accuracy problems are the book's: one internal contradiction on levonorgestrel and implantation (flag 1), six false or half-false "the chapter never covers" introductions (flag 3), an HPV statement that teaches warts lead to cancer (flag 4), overgeneral "only the condom" claims (flag 5), an overgeneral "both pills act by three mechanisms" (flag 6), an "equivalent" exponential formula that is not (flag 7) and small rounding slips (flag 8). Numbers, keys and counts are otherwise right, with Q7's "both false" key the one item needing a founder decision. The key points are accurate and already avoid most of the book's loose claims.
