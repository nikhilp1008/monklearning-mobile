One concept, taught start to finish — the board a student actually sees, and the teacher they actually hear. Switch teacher or language mid-class and it picks up from the same sentence in the other voice.

angular momentum — why the skater speeds up

Arms stretched out, she spins slowly. She pulls them in and speeds up — and nobody pushed her.

L = I ω

I = Σ m r2

Bring the mass inward and r drops. It is squared, so I drops much faster than r does.

no torque from outside ⇒ L cannot change:

I1ω1 = I2ω2

Arms out: I1 = 4 kg·m2, ω1 = 2 rev/s. Arms in: I2 = 1.2 kg·m2.

ω2 = (4 × 2) / 1.2 = 6.7 rev/s

remember: L needs an outside torque to change — but K = L²/2I goes UP. Her muscles did that work.

that's the whole concept — skater, diver, or a collapsing star: same equation.
