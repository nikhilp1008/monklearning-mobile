## Chemistry notation (new — this is the house form for every Chemistry chapter)

- **Element symbols, formulas and ions are upright** — never in `<i>`: H<sub>2</sub>O, CaCO<sub>3</sub>,
  SO<sub>4</sub><sup>2−</sup>, Fe<sup>3+</sup>, NH<sub>4</sub><sup>+</sup>, Cl<sup>−</sup>, e<sup>−</sup>. Charges in
  `<sup>` with the digit first and a real minus (U+2212): `<sup>2−</sup>`, `<sup>3+</sup>`, `<sup>+</sup>`.
  Coefficients plain, no space: 2H<sub>2</sub>O, 3NO<sub>3</sub><sup>−</sup> (keep the book's spacing if it
  prints a space). Hydrates as printed: CuSO<sub>4</sub>·5H<sub>2</sub>O.
- **State symbols** upright in brackets right after the formula: H<sub>2</sub>O(l), NaCl(aq), CO<sub>2</sub>(g).
  If the book sets them as subscripts, keep them as `<sub>(g)</sub>`-style only if it does; otherwise inline.
- **Isotopes / nuclide notation** (Ch 2): mass number and atomic number before the symbol, as printed:
  write `<sup>A</sup><sub>Z</sub>X` with the numbers, e.g. <sup>12</sup><sub>6</sub>C, <sup>35</sup>Cl. The
  two stack in the book; on one line they sit side by side — that is the "stacked maths" exception (no
  report needed for each, say once that you used this form).
- **Maths variables stay italic** as the book sets them: <i>n</i>, <i>M</i>, <i>V</i>, <i>m</i>, <i>T</i>, <i>P</i>,
  <i>λ</i>, <i>ν</i>, <i>h</i>, <i>c</i>, <i>k</i>, <i>Z</i> (atomic number as a variable), <i>x</i>, <i>y</i>.
  Label subscripts upright: <i>N</i><sub>A</sub>, <i>M</i><sub>r</sub>, <i>E</i><sub>n</sub> only if n is a label —
  here <i>n</i> is the variable, so <i>E</i><sub><i>n</i></sub>, <i>r</i><sub><i>n</i></sub>; <i>V</i><sub>(L)</sub> as
  printed; <i>R</i><sub>H</sub>, <i>a</i><sub>0</sub>, <i>v</i><sub>0</sub>, <i>m</i><sub>e</sub>, <i>K</i><sub>max</sub>,
  <i>ν</i><sub>0</sub>, <i>W</i><sub>0</sub>, <i>n</i><sub>1</sub>, <i>n</i><sub>2</sub>.
- **Quantum numbers** (Ch 2): <i>n</i>, <i>l</i>, <i>m</i><sub><i>l</i></sub>, <i>m</i><sub>s</sub> as the book sets them
  (check the image: if <i>m</i><sub>s</sub>'s s is italic in the book, keep it italic). Orbital and subshell
  labels are upright letters: 1s, 2p, 3d, 4f; configurations 1s<sup>2</sup> 2s<sup>2</sup> 2p<sup>6</sup>,
  [Ar]3d<sup>6</sup>4s<sup>2</sup>. BUT the book often sets these in maths italic (3𝑑, 4𝑠 in the dump):
  look at the page image and write them as the NCERT convention, upright, consistently — and say so once in
  your report. Orbital subscripts: p<sub><i>x</i></sub>, d<sub><i>xy</i></sub>, d<sub><i>z</i>²</sub>,
  d<sub><i>x</i>²−<i>y</i>²</sub> (Unicode superscript ² inside `<sub>`, because `<sup>` inside `<sub>` is
  flattened). ψ is italic like the book's other maths letters (<i>ψ</i>, <i>ψ</i><sup>2</sup>,
  4π<i>r</i><sup>2</sup><i>ψ</i><sup>2</sup>); π is always upright.
- **Reaction arrows:** → (U+2192) for a one-way reaction, whatever its length in print (never ⟶);
  ⇌ (U+21CC) for equilibrium, exactly where the book prints a double arrow. **A condition written over an
  arrow** (Δ, hν, a catalyst, a temperature) goes in `<sup>` right after the arrow, no spaces:
  `CaCO<sub>3</sub> →<sup>Δ</sup> CaO + CO<sub>2</sub>`, `→<sup>Fe, 700 K</sup>`. A condition under the arrow goes
  in `<sub>` right after it (after the `<sup>` if both). Report every arrow condition longer than ~12
  characters so the Android check can look at it.
- Units upright with a space: g mol<sup>−1</sup>, mol L<sup>−1</sup>, kJ mol<sup>−1</sup>, J s, m s<sup>−1</sup>, °C, K,
  pm, nm, Å. The book's errata note that Ch 1's cheat sheet uses a hyphen in "g mL-1": write the real minus
  and report it.
- Bond/structure dashes in text (C–H, Cl–Cl) use an en dash as printed; double/triple bonds = and ≡.
- **Structures, apparatus, orbital pictures, energy-level diagrams, spectra and graphs are drawings** →
  diagram ids. A table is never a drawing. If a structure cannot be drawn faithfully, the drawer reports it
  rather than approximating.
- **Recompute every number**: molar masses (with the book's atomic masses), moles, percentages, empirical
  formulas, limiting reagents, concentrations, n-factors and equivalent masses, wavelengths, energies,
  Bohr radii and energies, numbers of spectral lines, unpaired electrons. Report any that do not come out.
- Significant figures: the book's answers often carry a sig-fig claim — check each against Topic 03's own

  error, not a nit).
- Typos the errata already list as corrected must be as the errata says ("Now: …"): check, e.g. Ch 1 p. 22
  exercise 3 (NH<sub>3</sub>), p. 28 exercise 5 (0.055 mol CO / 0.065 mol CO<sub>2</sub>), p. 29 MCQ Q4
  (2 g H<sub>2</sub>, 32 g O<sub>2</sub>, 8 g He, 23 g Na), Ch 2 nuclear diameter (10<sup>−13</sup> to
  10<sup>−12</sup> cm), Ch 2 de Broglie MCQ Q3 explanation, Ch 2 cheat sheet mnemonic removed. (The errata's
