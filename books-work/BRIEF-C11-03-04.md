## Chemistry drawings (new for this pair)

- **The periodic table (Figure 3.1)** is a real drawing: draw the long form from data (every element's
  symbol at its group/period, lanthanoids/actinoids below), with the book's labels and highlights; the
  `check` verifies every symbol's position against a table of Z → (group, period) and the block colouring
  against the electron configuration rule. If it cannot be legible on a phone at full size, follow the
  diagram brief's "fitting matters" rule (a faithful schematic, said in the report) — never drop elements
  the book shows.
- **Trend graphs (Figure 3.2)**: plot the book's own values (first ionisation enthalpies, kJ mol⁻¹, for
  Z = 1–20 — use the standard NCERT data and check that the book's peaks and dips are where the data puts
  them).
- **Structures** (ozone, azide, SF₄, diborane, nitrophenols, NH₄⁺): atoms as element symbols, bonds as lines
  (double/triple as parallel lines), lone pairs as dot pairs, formal charges as small ⊕/⊖ or ⁺/⁻ as printed,
  dotted lines for hydrogen bonds, arrows for coordinate bonds as the book draws them. Geometry computed
  (bond angles from VSEPR — 120°, 109.5°, trigonal-bipyramidal axial/equatorial — or the book's stated
  angles). The `check` verifies: every atom's valence electron count, formal charges sum to the stated
  charge, the electron total, the stated bond angles and lengths.
- **Energy diagrams** (Born–Haber cycle, H₂ potential energy curve, any MO diagram): computed from the
  values the book gives (the Born–Haber step energies must sum by Hess's law to ΔfH, with the lattice step
  written −ΔlatticeH as the errata says; the H₂ curve's minimum at the stated bond length and energy).

## Lessons from the Chapter 1–2 reviews (follow them)

- **The Formula sheet never shows `note`.** Every symbol a formula uses goes in its `legend`
  (`symbol = meaning`); a condition the formula needs to be read right ("for 1 ≤ N < 10") also goes in the
  legend. `note` is only for remarks.
- **Formula kickers mark their own maths with `<i>`** (Chemistry titles no longer guess).
- **Answers printed twice:** both reviewers caught examples whose last step and `ans` both state the
  result. Cut the step before the result.
- **Book "the chapter never covers / omits / shows" claims** were false about half the time in Ch 1–2.
  Check each one against the topics and report.
- **MCQ nudges that describe the wrong option** were the commonest book error: recompute what each wrong
  option actually is and check its nudge says that.
- **Notation settled in Ch 1–2:** α, γ italic (as the book's maths); orbital labels upright (1s, 2p, 3d,
  p<sub><i>x</i></sub>); state symbols upright; label subscripts upright, variable subscripts italic;
  memory aids open with “ (the book often prints ” — fix and list).
- **Recompute** every formal charge, oxidation state, bond order, number of unpaired electrons, hybridisation,
  bond angle, dipole-moment conclusion, number of σ/π bonds and lone pairs, ionisation/electron-gain value
  comparison and lattice-energy sign.
