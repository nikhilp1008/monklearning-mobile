## Suspected errors in the book (copied as printed)
1. **p. 309, T02 MCQ Q1, option (d) "2 and 16".**
   - **Book's nudge:** "(d) doubled everything unnecessarily and mis-set H⁺."
   - **Why it is wrong:** doubling the balanced equation gives 10 Fe²⁺ and 16 H⁺, not 2 and 16. "2 and 16" are the MnO₄⁻ and H⁺ coefficients of the oxalate equation in example 2. The nudge does not describe its option.
   - **Suggested fix:** "(d) borrows the 2 and 16 of the permanganate–oxalate equation — 2 is the MnO₄⁻ coefficient there, not Fe²⁺."
   - **Confidence:** fairly high.
2. **p. 314, T03 Section 3B, Step 2.**
   - **Book's text:** "the lower-E° half-cell becomes the anode (forced into oxidation, its E° sign reverses)".
   - **Why it is a problem:** Step 3 then subtracts the unreversed reduction potential. A student who reverses the sign and then subtracts double-counts, the classic sign error.
   - **Suggested fix:** "(forced into oxidation; keep its reduction E° for Step 3)".
   - **Confidence:** medium.
3. **p. 313, T03 Limiting conditions (2).**
   - **Book's text:** "E°cell > 0 (equivalently ΔG < 0)".
   - **Why it is wrong:** it pairs a standard quantity with a non-standard one. It should read E°cell > 0 ⇔ ΔG° < 0, or E_cell > 0 ⇔ ΔG < 0.
   - **Confidence:** low (an imprecision).
4. **p. 314, T03 Units.**
   - **Book's text:** "equivalents, n, and K are dimensionless".
   - **Why it is a problem:** equivalents are an amount of substance (eq, like mol), so they are not dimensionless.
   - **Confidence:** low.
5. **Chapter consistency, for the c7b writer and the reviewer.**
   - T03 (pp. 313–314) gives standard conditions as "1 bar gases".
   - Additional topic A (p. 327) says "1 atm pressure for any gas".
6. **p. 299, T01 example 2.**
   - **Book's text:** "between the negative hydride and the positive oxoacids".
   - **Why it is a problem:** this calls HCl a "hydride". That is loose wording for hydrogen chloride.
   - **Confidence:** low.
